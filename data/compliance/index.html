COMPLIANCE CENTER

Controls before commerce.

Provenance

Source, collection method, transformations and rights documentation.

Consent & lawful basis

Document the basis supporting the intended collection, use and licensing.

Sensitive-data screening

Flag or block credentials, children's data and other high-risk categories by default.

Jurisdiction engine

Availability is evaluated against configurable country/state rules rather than assumed globally.

Audit trail

Record material listing, access, license, rights and takedown events.

Launch gate

Legal, privacy, security, tax, payments and licensing review remain required before production launch.

Important: These controls do not by themselves establish legal compliance. Rollin must obtain appropriate professional review and required registrations or licenses before operating regulated features in a jurisdiction.
