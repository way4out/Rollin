Rollin Terms

Effective September 30, 2026.

Marketplace role

Rollin provides marketplace technology for listings, communications, offers, auctions and eligible payment processing. Rollin does not itself transfer title, ownership, licenses, registrations, permits or possession of an asset.

Seller responsibility

Sellers must provide accurate information and must have the right to offer each item. Sellers are responsible for applicable title, registration, licensing, taxes, disclosures, inspections, export/import rules and other legal requirements.

Buyer responsibility

Buyers are responsible for reviewing listings, completing appropriate diligence and confirming that a transaction is permitted where they live and where the asset is located.

Restricted transactions

Rollin may reject, remove, suspend or cancel listings or accounts involving prohibited, unlawful, fraudulent, unsafe or insufficiently documented transactions.

Payments

Eligible card and other payment methods are processed by third-party payment providers. Availability depends on provider, account, currency, geography and transaction eligibility. Seller payouts require successful payment-provider onboarding and verification.

Disputes and refunds

Payment disputes, refunds and reversals are handled under the applicable transaction terms and payment-provider rules. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

No universal legality promise

A listing being visible on Rollin does not mean the transaction is legal, licensed or permitted in every jurisdiction.

Contact

Marketplace operator: StellarNet LLC. Use the Contact page for support.

These terms are operational marketplace language, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
