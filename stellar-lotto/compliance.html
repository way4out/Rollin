Activation rule

ALL gates must be verified and current. Missing, expired, revoked, conflicting or stale evidence keeps paid play disabled. Client-side assertions cannot override server-side authorization.

Required evidence includes actual operator authorization, jurisdiction-specific legal review, server geolocation, approved age/KYC/AML providers, responsible-play controls, independent contract audit, recognized verifiable randomness and audited settlement infrastructure.

No fabricated credentials

This application does not claim a gambling license, audit, certification, KYC approval, AML registration or regulator authorization unless the underlying evidence has actually been supplied and independently verified.

Technical security requirements

  • Server-authoritative authorization decisions.
  • Re-check jurisdiction at sensitive lifecycle events.
  • VPN/proxy/location-integrity controls.
  • Immutable compliance/audit events.
  • Multisig + timelock + emergency pause for contract administration.
  • Bytecode-to-audited-source verification.
  • Public randomness proof and reproducible winner calculation.
  • One-time claim state, replay protection and reconciliation.
