Stellar Lotto Legal & Compliance

Paid play: LOCKED

Stellar Lotto operates in FAIL-CLOSED mode. No paid entry, deposit, wager, prize claim, or payout is enabled from this public shell until the applicable operator authorization and jurisdiction-specific controls are verified.

Required production gates

  1. Verified operator authorization for every enabled jurisdiction.
  2. Jurisdiction rules maintained from authoritative legal/compliance review.
  3. Server-side geolocation decision at registration, login, entry, draw and withdrawal; client signals are not sufficient.
  4. Age and identity verification using an approved provider where required.
  5. KYC, sanctions and AML controls, transaction monitoring and investigation workflow where legally required.
  6. Responsible-play controls: self-exclusion, cooling-off, limits, history, alerts and anti-circumvention controls.
  7. Independent smart-contract security audit before contracts hold customer funds.
  8. Independently verifiable randomness with publicly reproducible proof.
  9. On-chain ticket, draw, claim and settlement records with reconciliation and one-time payout protection.
  10. Legal terms, privacy disclosures, game rules, odds/prize disclosures and jurisdiction restrictions reviewed for each launch market.

California

DENIED. This application does not enable online lottery/gambling activity in California. California's Gambling Control Commission states that it does not issue licenses to online casinos and that online gambling is illegal in California. California's 2025 AB 831 also changed California rules concerning online sweepstakes and gambling simulators effective January 1, 2026.

This site therefore makes no claim that Stellar Lotto is licensed or authorized in California.

Transparency

Production contracts, independent audit reports, randomness proofs, operator authorization, supported jurisdictions, responsible-play controls and settlement records must be published before paid play is enabled. No audit, license, certification or regulatory approval is represented by this page unless an actual document is supplied and verified.
