Restricted & Prohibited Transactions

Rollin does not permit unlawful goods or services, fraud, stolen property, counterfeit goods, controlled substances, prohibited weapons, exploitative content, or transactions that violate applicable sanctions, export controls or marketplace/payment-provider rules.

Regulated categories such as aircraft, boats, vehicles, real estate and businesses may require additional ownership, title, registration, licensing, inspection, disclosure or jurisdiction-specific documentation before a transaction can proceed.

Rollin may request documentation, restrict a listing, suspend an account or decline a transaction when eligibility cannot be established.
