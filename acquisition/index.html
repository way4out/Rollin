Technology and commercial diligence priorities

  • Revenue: TTM revenue, recurring revenue, gross margin, EBITDA/SDE, customer concentration and payment-provider statements.
  • Telecom: carrier/MVNO relationships, eSIM provisioning, SIM inventory, numbering, roaming, regulatory registrations and vendor contracts.
  • Blockchain: token contracts, issuer/control rights, treasury wallets, payment flows, smart contracts, chain dependencies and audit history.
  • Software: repository ownership, commit history, deployment accounts, secrets rotation, open-source licenses, dependencies and third-party APIs.
  • Marketplace: listings, sellers/buyers, transaction records, payment rails, disputes, moderation, privacy and consumer-protection obligations.
  • Data: user-consent records, privacy policies, retention, data-processing agreements and lawful transferability of customer information.
  • IP: trademarks, copyrights, patents/applications if any, domains, designs, source code and contractor invention-assignment agreements.
  • Liabilities: taxes, debts, refunds, chargebacks, litigation, contracts, licenses, regulatory exposure and contingent obligations.

Transaction structure

A buyer can evaluate an asset purchase, equity/membership-interest transaction, or another mutually agreed structure after legal and tax advice. The definitive agreement should identify every included asset, excluded asset, assumed liability, retained liability, transition obligation, IP assignment, account transfer and closing condition.

No automatic inclusion: A product, domain, token, wallet, repository, user account, contract, physical device, claim or intellectual-property right is not represented as included merely because it is associated with the StellarNet ecosystem. The signed asset schedule controls.
