Sale strategy: run a controlled strategic process

The implementation is designed around one principle: reduce buyer uncertainty before asking the buyer to price the opportunity. Current M&A guidance emphasizes early data-room preparation, staged disclosure, buyer qualification, defined process deadlines and careful technical/IP diligence. Acquire seller process guidance · Orrick technology-company sale guidance.

Phase 1 — Ready (Days 0–7): freeze the definitive asset list; reconcile ownership; inventory domains, repositories, deployments, contracts, wallets/payment systems and telecom dependencies; assemble corporate and financial records; identify gaps.
Phase 2 — Prove (Days 7–14): build a buyer-ready one-page CIM, financial snapshot, product demo, technical architecture, asset schedule, diligence index, FAQ and known-issues register. Never convert an unverified claim into a fact.
Phase 3 — Qualify (Days 14–30): outreach to strategic buyer classes; record every inquiry; verify identity, acquisition rationale, decision authority and funding capability; use NDA before sensitive disclosure.
Phase 4 — Compete fairly (Days 21–45): give qualified buyers the same core information and process timetable; solicit indications/LOIs; compare total economics, certainty of close, conditions, escrow/holdback and assumed liabilities rather than headline price alone.
Phase 5 — Close (typically after LOI): open deeper diligence; resolve technical/legal/financial findings; negotiate definitive agreement; obtain required consents; execute escrow/closing and controlled account/IP/domain transfer.

Negotiation rules

  • Do not invent bids, revenue, customers, approvals, licenses or deadlines.
  • Do not release private keys, credentials, sensitive personal data or unrestricted source code through the public page.
  • Do not grant exclusivity before a serious LOI and counsel review.
  • Compare offers on cash at close, contingencies, escrow, liabilities assumed, taxes, transition obligations and closing certainty.
  • Document every included/excluded asset in schedules.
  • Use transaction counsel and tax professionals for binding documents and structure.
