Recommended sale strategy

The sale should be run as a structured strategic acquisition process, not simply as a public “for sale” advertisement. The objective is to make the opportunity easy to understand, progressively proveable, and straightforward to close while protecting confidential information.

1 · Package the asset. Create one master asset schedule covering software, domains, repositories, telecom/eSIM work, marketplace systems, blockchain/payment integrations, brand/documentation and any related initiatives. Mark every item included, excluded, licensed, third-party, or subject to verification.
2 · Lead with a buyer thesis. Present StellarNet as an integrated technology acquisition opportunity rather than assigning unsupported values to individual concepts. Show what a buyer can verify now and what requires diligence.
3 · Qualify buyers. Prioritize strategic operators, telecom/connectivity companies, software companies, digital-asset/payment businesses and technology investors whose existing infrastructure could reduce integration time. Verify identity, authority and ability to fund before releasing sensitive information.
4 · Use staged disclosure. Public page → buyer questionnaire → NDA → controlled data room → management call/demo → indication/LOI → confirmatory diligence → definitive agreement → escrow/closing.
5 · Create competitive tension without false urgency. Invite qualified parties into the same process and use clear response dates. Do not claim competing offers or deadlines unless they actually exist.
6 · Negotiate total consideration, not headline price alone. Compare cash at close, escrow/holdback, earnout, assumed liabilities, transition services, retained assets, taxes and closing conditions together.
