Rollin Domain Authorization

This page documents deployment authorization for domains connected to Rollin. It does not create, transfer, register, or bypass registrar ownership, payment, identity, DNS, hosting, or provider safeguards.

Owner authorization

The repository owner may publish Rollin application code to hosting accounts and domains they control, subject to provider terms and applicable law.

Required for a real domain

  • Authorized registrar account
  • Domain registration and verified registrant information where required
  • DNS control
  • Hosting authorization
  • Applicable business, privacy, consumer, payments, tax, and other compliance controls
