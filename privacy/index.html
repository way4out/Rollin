Rollin Privacy

Effective September 30, 2026.

Information used

Rollin may process account details, listing information, messages, transaction records, payment status, shipping information and technical information needed to operate the marketplace.

Payment information

Eligible card payments are handled by payment providers such as Stripe. Rollin is not intended to store full payment-card credentials.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, listings, purchases, sales, payments, fraud prevention, security, support and required operational communications.

Service providers

Rollin may use infrastructure and payment providers to provide marketplace services. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

Retention and requests

Records may be retained as reasonably necessary for transactions, security, legal obligations and dispute handling. Contact Rollin to request access, correction or deletion where applicable.

Contact

Marketplace operator: StellarNet LLC. Use the Contact page.

This page describes the current product approach and is not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.
