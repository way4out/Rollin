Resolver layer

Keep ordinary HTTPS/DNS as a compatibility path while supporting decentralized name resolution where the client and resolver support it.

OEQL layer

oeql:// is treated as an application/deep-link scheme. It requires an installed handler or compatible client; it is not assumed to be a universal browser protocol.

Portfolio ledger

Track each name by exact name, registry, registration transaction, controller, status, resolver target and canonical HTTPS destination.

Safety gate

Never overwrite an existing registrant, misrepresent ownership, or bypass registry rules. Brand, trademark, sanctions, jurisdiction and content checks remain part of acquisition.

Ownership model

StellarNet LLC can be the business owner/controller for names actually acquired by the company. Personal registrations can remain under the individual's account where appropriate. Keep registration credentials and signing keys under direct authorized control.

This implementation is infrastructure and software, not a legal transfer instrument or declaration of title.
