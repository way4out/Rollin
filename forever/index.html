An unbounded OEQL naming and control layer. Protocol use is free; issued names are governed by explicit authorization records and, where applicable, lease/license terms.

Generate an identifier

Identifiers are generated on demand. There is no pre-created list and no finite namespace counter.

Unbounded namespace

Names can be extended by hierarchy, path, version, dimension, and content-addressed suffixes. Capacity is constrained by the implementation, storage, cryptography, and applicable systems—not by a hardcoded total count.

Lease, not sale

Where StellarNet actually controls issuance, a record can grant a renewable license/lease while retaining underlying protocol and namespace rights.

Up to 100 signers

Authorization records support 1–100 signers, with same-name constraints, timestamps, expiry, revocation, and a canonical record hash.

Portable resolution

oeql://name.forever is the canonical application form. HTTPS fallback remains available where a device has no registered OEQL handler.

Authorization record builder

This creates a deterministic local draft. A legally binding agreement still requires the parties, applicable terms, and an actual acceptance/signature mechanism.

Rights boundary

Strongest deployable rule: StellarNet retains all rights it actually owns or controls and grants only the rights expressly authorized by the applicable record.

Not created by deployment alone: global ownership of the DNS root, an ICANN TLD, an IANA URI-scheme registration, another party's domain, trademark, or infrastructure.
