# .forever Registry — Maximum-Control Readiness Plan

## Objective
Prepare StellarNet LLC to pursue the strongest lawful control available over the `.forever` string and its registry operation.

## Important status
The public Rollin/OEQL software cannot by itself create ownership of a DNS top-level domain. ICANN's 2026 New gTLD Applicant Journey requires application, evaluation, contracting, technical onboarding, and delegation.

The 2026 application submission window closed on 12 August 2026 at 23:59 UTC. This document is therefore a readiness package, not a claim that an ICANN application has been filed.

## Control objective
If StellarNet LLC becomes the approved registry operator and the string is delegated, the production control plane SHOULD be structured so StellarNet controls the registry operations permitted by the registry agreement, registry policy within applicable rules, authoritative registry workflow, operational credentials, DNS service configuration, security keys, renewal/transfer processes, abuse/compliance operations, and audit records.

## Corporate readiness
Confirm before filing or contracting:
- StellarNet LLC legal-entity records
- authorized signatory
- financial capability
- technical registry provider
- DNS/anycast/DDoS architecture
- DNSSEC/key-management plan
- abuse and compliance operations
- privacy/data-protection program
- registrar/registry integrations
- business continuity and escrow arrangements
- insurance and professional-advisor review
- trademark and string-risk analysis

## Rights model
The registry agreement and applicable law control actual TLD rights. OEQL software licenses and internal lease records cannot supersede them.

## Current implementation
Rollin/OEQL supports an application-level `.forever` namespace now and is architected so that a future delegated DNS registry can be separated from the application presentation layer.

## No false filing claim
No ICANN application, registry agreement, delegation, or global TLD ownership is claimed until the relevant official record exists and is independently verifiable.
