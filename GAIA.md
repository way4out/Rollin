# Rollin Gaia Charter

Rollin is designed around People, Planet and Prosperity.

- Human agency: clear choices, informed consent, accessible controls and easy cancellation.
- Privacy: collect only what is necessary and protect account and wallet data.
- Accessibility: design for varied abilities, devices, languages and connection quality.
- Sustainability: optimize data transfer, rendering, storage and infrastructure; measure impact rather than making unsupported claims.
- Honest commerce: no fabricated scarcity, balances, confirmations or guaranteed financial outcomes.
- Durable value: prioritize products, services, memberships and merchant utility that create repeat use because they are useful.

The public W3C Web Sustainability Guidelines emphasize people, planet and prosperity, including accessibility, privacy, performance, efficient infrastructure and measurable impact.
